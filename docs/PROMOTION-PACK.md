## Editorial submission / Suggested Post approach

Do not send the same generic advert to multiple channels. First verify that the channel accepts free Suggested Posts or editorial contributions. Then adapt one genuinely useful item to its audience.

Платное размещение не предлагаю и массовую рекламу не рассылаю. Если такой формат вам подходит, подготовлю материал под аудиторию канала.

### Editorial post structure

1. One precise headline describing the event, not a promise or price prediction.
2. Two or three short sentences: what happened and what is confirmed.
3. One sentence explaining why it matters to crypto/Web3 readers.
4. A clear caveat for unconfirmed plans, forecasts, yields or project claims.
5. One primary source link and, where useful, one independent report.
6. A discreet channel credit only where the destination permits it.

The post should be useful even if the reader never subscribes to CryptoPing RU. No referral links, forced subscriptions, giveaway promises or urgency hooks.

## Current channel-quality constraint

The public audit sampled 100 posts across 55 hours and saw a median of 2 views per post with about 43.6 posts/day. Several stories appeared in near-duplicate form. Do not use a directory/partnership submission as a reason to increase this volume. Fix duplication and editorial value first.

## Manual-only routes

Some directories require a normal Telegram client, a browser anti-bot check, or hand-reviewed email suggestions. Do not automate around these controls:
- TGbox: free submission via its Telegram bot DM; user must send the public channel URL from a normal Telegram account.
- TeleNavo: free web form, but Cloudflare Turnstile is present; privacy policy says submitter IP is stored. Submit manually from a normal browser only if the user is comfortable with that.
- TGCompass: no open automatic submit form; suggestions to its editorial email are hand-curated and most do not make the short lists.
