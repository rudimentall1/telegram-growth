## Owner's account-safety and publishing rules

- Use Telegram's official client or supported publishing interfaces. Bot API use must remain within Telegram's actual documented limits.
- The often-cited 30 messages/second is a platform ceiling, **not a target**. Actual chat-specific limits and Telegram flood-control responses take precedence.
- No unsolicited promotional DMs and no mass messaging unrelated chats.
- Avoid sudden waves of identical advertising posts with external links across multiple groups.
- Do not assume a group is open, unmoderated, or accepts arbitrary referral links from a directory description or this candidate list.
- Match the post to the group's actual topic and rules. Keep referral links only where the destination explicitly permits them.
- For editorial bots / channel submissions, prepare unique, useful news, analysis or guides. Do not send generic "subscribe to us" pitches.
- Investigate a channel's editorial submission or Suggested Posts route separately from its admin-only channel posting permissions.

## Verification workflow

For each candidate, record: exact public URL/handle, active status, current rules, whether ordinary members can post, whether bots are permitted, referral-link policy, promotion thread/cadence, free vs paid terms, and evidence/date checked. Keep candidate status separate from permission status. Only enable Bot API delivery when actual membership and live send permission exist and current rules support the specific content. Human-account participation remains manual and must follow the same rules.
