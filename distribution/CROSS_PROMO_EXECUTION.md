# CryptoPing RU: free cross-promotion execution checklist

**Prepared:** 2026-10-31  
**Scope:** opt-in, no-cash Telegram post swaps only. This document does not authorize a platform connection, grant channel permissions, accept a campaign, or publish a post.

## Priority

1. **Onflow Ads Cross-Promotion** — first platform to evaluate. Official guide describes free 1:1 post swaps. Free plan currently allows up to five campaigns started per calendar month; joining another owner's campaign is not capped. Requires an account, verified email, a public channel, and its bot connected as channel admin. Confirm the exact permission request in the product before granting anything.
2. **Trigram Cross-Promotion** — second platform to evaluate. Official site describes free swaps matched by language, topic and subscriber range, with simultaneous bot publication and a one-day live period. Connect only through the cross-promotion path; do not enable paid listings or ad sales.
3. **Adpact Cross-Promotion** — third platform to evaluate. No cash payment is required, but swaps consume in-app points. Points are not money and cannot be withdrawn. Review the exact point cost, partner and post terms in the Mini App before accepting; acceptance is not cancellable under the current guide.

Official references:
- https://docs.onflowads.com/cross-promo
- https://docs.onflowads.com/cross-promo/faq

- https://adpact.app/guides/telegram-cross-promotion/

## Non-negotiable gates

- Only use the platform's explicit cross-promotion / post-swap feature.
- No paid placements, boosts, subscriptions, cash top-ups or paid extras.
- Do not connect a bot until the channel owner has reviewed the requested permissions. Grant only the minimum permissions the chosen workflow explicitly requires. Never grant ownership, invite-user, manage-admin or unrelated rights.
- Do not accept a partner based on subscriber count alone. Compare recent average post views, language, topic, content quality and audience overlap.
- Reject partners with obvious engagement manipulation, unrelated content, investment-profit promises, or suspiciously inconsistent reach.
- Review both final posts, links and schedule before accepting. Never approve a post that changes after review.
- Limit initial test exposure to one swap. Do not enable recurring campaigns until the first result is measured.

## Measurement protocol

Before the first swap, record:
- channel subscriber count;
- median and average views for the latest 10 comparable posts;
- baseline join/leave counts from the existing growth report;
- date and time of the baseline.

For each accepted swap, record:
- platform and campaign ID;
- partner channel and its recent average views;
- approved post text and final link;
- scheduled time and agreed live duration;
- unique invite-link source, if the existing bot can create and observe it;
- post views at 24 hours and 7 days;
- attributed joins, observed leaves and D7 retention when available.

Telegram Bot API invite-link attribution measures observed joins, not clicks. If source attribution is unavailable, record it as unavailable. Do not infer clicks or claim a placement caused every channel-level subscriber change.

## Decision rule

Run one controlled swap at a time. Compare attributed joins and retention with the partner's actual reach and the channel's baseline. Repeat only when the exchange produces relevant, retained subscribers without harming the channel's normal content performance.
